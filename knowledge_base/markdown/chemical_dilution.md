# Chemical Dilution SOP
Category: chemical dilution
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Chemical Dilution

## Approved Chemicals
- Use only chemicals approved for the site and task.
- Check the product label before use.
- Do not use an unlabelled bottle.
- Do not bring in personal cleaning chemicals.

## Dilution Rules
- Follow the site dilution chart or dispensing system.
- Do not guess chemical ratios.
- Add chemical only as directed by the product label or dispensing system.
- Never mix chemicals together.
- Ask the supervisor if the correct dilution is not available.

## Storage
- Keep chemicals in labelled containers.
- Store chemicals in the approved storage area.
- Keep chemical containers closed when not in use.
