# Floor Scrubbing SOP
Category: floor scrubbing
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Floor Scrubbing

## Preparation
- Inspect the floor for hazards before using a scrubber.
- Remove loose debris before wet cleaning.
- Place wet floor signs at all entrances to the work area.
- Check that the machine, pads, squeegee, and cables are safe to use.

## Scrubbing Procedure
- Use the approved floor cleaning chemical at the dilution shown on the site chart.
- Keep the machine moving to avoid damaging the floor.
- Do not leave pooled water on the floor.
- Recover dirty solution with the scrubber or wet vacuum.

## Completion
- Empty and rinse recovery tanks after use.
- Clean pads, brushes, and squeegees.
- Store the machine in the approved storage area.
- Leave warning signs in place until the floor is fully dry.
