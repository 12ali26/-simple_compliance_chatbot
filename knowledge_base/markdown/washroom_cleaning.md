# Washroom Cleaning SOP
Category: washroom cleaning
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Washroom Cleaning

## Frequency And Checks
- Check public washrooms at the frequency set by the site schedule.
- Record each completed check on the washroom inspection sheet.
- Report blocked toilets, leaks, broken dispensers, or missing supplies to the supervisor.

## PPE
- Wear disposable gloves before starting washroom cleaning.
- Wear eye protection if there is a risk of splashing.
- Change gloves after handling waste or contaminated items.

## Chemical Usage
- Use only the site-approved washroom disinfectant or toilet cleaner.
- Follow the product label and site dilution chart before using any chemical.
- Never mix chemicals.
- Do not guess dilution ratios; ask the supervisor if the chart is missing or unclear.

## Cleaning Steps
- Place the wet floor sign before cleaning starts.
- Remove waste and replace bin liners.
- Clean high-touch points including handles, taps, flush buttons, rails, and dispensers.
- Clean sinks, counters, toilets, urinals, and surrounding surfaces.
- Mop the floor last and keep the wet floor sign in place until the floor is dry.
