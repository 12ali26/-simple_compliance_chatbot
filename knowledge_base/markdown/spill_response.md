# Spill Response SOP
Category: spill response
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Spill Response

## General Spill Safety
- Stop work and prevent people from walking through the spill area.
- Place warning signs or barriers around the spill.
- Wear the PPE required for the spill type before cleanup starts.
- Do not touch unknown spills until the supervisor confirms the correct response.

## Blood Or Body Fluid Spill
- Wear disposable gloves, apron, and eye or face protection.
- Cover the spill with approved absorbent material.
- Apply the approved disinfectant using the contact time stated on the product label.
- Remove absorbent material and contaminated waste into the approved waste bag.
- Clean and disinfect the area again after the spill material is removed.
- Wash hands after removing PPE.

## Chemical Spill
- Do not clean a chemical spill unless trained and authorized.
- Keep people away from the area.
- Inform the supervisor immediately.
- Follow the site chemical spill procedure and safety data sheet.
