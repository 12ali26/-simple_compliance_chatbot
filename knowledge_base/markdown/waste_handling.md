# Waste Handling SOP
Category: waste handling
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Waste Handling

## Routine Waste
- Wear disposable gloves when handling waste.
- Do not press waste down by hand.
- Tie bags securely before removing them from bins.
- Replace bin liners after waste is removed.
- Move waste using the approved trolley where available.

## Contaminated Waste
- Place contaminated cleaning materials in the approved waste bag.
- Do not mix contaminated waste with routine general waste.
- Report leaking bags or incorrect waste containers to the supervisor.

## Sharps
- Do not pick up needles or sharps by hand.
- Keep people away from the area.
- Report sharps immediately to the supervisor or trained person.
