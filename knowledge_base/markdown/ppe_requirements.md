# PPE Requirements SOP
Category: PPE requirements
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - PPE Requirements

## Standard Cleaning PPE
- Wear disposable gloves for routine cleaning tasks.
- Wear closed footwear with non-slip soles.
- Wear an apron when clothing may be contaminated.
- Wash or sanitize hands after removing gloves.

## Splash Risk
- Wear eye protection or a face visor when cleaning may cause splashing.
- Use splash protection when handling body fluid spills or strong cleaning chemicals.

## Isolation Areas
- Follow the isolation room sign and supervisor instructions before entry.
- Wear the PPE listed for the isolation room.
- Remove PPE before leaving the isolation area unless the site procedure says otherwise.
- Perform hand hygiene immediately after removing PPE.
