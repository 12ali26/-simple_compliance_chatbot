# Isolation Room Cleaning SOP
Category: isolation room cleaning
Source: Sample Approved Procedure - Isolation Room Cleaning

## Before Entry
- Check the isolation sign and confirm required PPE before entering.
- Gather all approved cleaning supplies before entry.
- Do not enter if the required PPE is unavailable.
- Ask the supervisor if isolation requirements are unclear.

## Cleaning Method
- Clean from cleaner areas toward dirtier areas.
- Clean high-touch points including bed rails, call bells, handles, switches, taps, and bathroom fixtures.
- Use the approved disinfectant and follow the contact time on the product label.
- Change cloths when moving between dirty and clean areas.

## Leaving The Room
- Remove waste according to the isolation waste procedure.
- Remove PPE in the approved order before leaving the area.
- Perform hand hygiene immediately after removing PPE.
- Report missing supplies, damaged equipment, or unclear instructions to the supervisor.
